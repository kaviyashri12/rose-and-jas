📖 How Secure Messaging Works

1️⃣ Encryption

Your message is encrypted using Receiver's Public Key. Only the receiver can decrypt it.

2️⃣ Digital Signature

You sign the message using Your Private Key. This proves it's really from you.

3️⃣ Secure Transmission

Both encrypted message and signature are sent together to the receiver.

4️⃣ Verification

Receiver decrypts with their private key and verifies your signature with your public key.

🔒 Security Tips:
  • Never share your Private Key with anyone
  • Share your Public Key freely so others can send you encrypted messages
  • Always verify the receiver's username before sending
  • For maximum security, use 2048-bit or higher keys
